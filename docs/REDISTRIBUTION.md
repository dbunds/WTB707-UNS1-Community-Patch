# Redistribution and provenance

The public source lineage is Microsoft's [msfs-avionics-mirror](https://github.com/microsoft/msfs-avionics-mirror), published under modified MIT terms that restrict use of the software and its derivatives to Microsoft Flight Simulator and require retention of the copyright and license notices.

The tested release targets the newer shipped Boeing 707 WT2.2.4 instrument, not a complete rebuild of the public 2.1.11 snapshot. The maintainer reported obtaining approval to redistribute the modified shipped instrument on November 25, 2026. The actual permission correspondence is not reproduced here. This record is not a claim of endorsement or blanket permission to redistribute other simulator files.

Accordingly, v0.1.0 uses a normal Community-folder ZIP with the original tested package intact, plus the upstream license, attribution notice and installation instructions. The experimental local patch-generation route is superseded for this release.

Keep the applicable licensing notices with redistribution, distribute only for MSFS use, and reassess provenance and permission for future upstream versions or additional assets. See [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).
